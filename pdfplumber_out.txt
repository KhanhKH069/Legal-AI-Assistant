FloodSOS – AI-Powered Flood Rescue Coordination System 11/2025 - NOW
View project
- FloodSOS is an AI-powered flood rescue coordination ecosystem developed for Central Vietnam, integrating
multi-resolution flood probability prediction, automated SOS urgency scoring, and adaptive flood-aware routing
(FASR) to streamline end-to-end rescue dispatching.
- The system combines physics-informed machine learning (PINNs/FNO surrogate models built on HEC-RAS/MIKE
hydraulic simulations) with real-time weather data to provide 7-day flood risk forecasting down to commune level
across 32 flood-prone communes in Nghe An province.
- A command dashboard and offline-capable mobile app connect three user groups — citizens sending SOS
signals, coordination centers prioritizing and dispatching tasks, and rescue teams executing missions — forming a
closed-loop rescue workflow from signal intake to mission completion.
- The core algorithmic innovation, Flood-Aware Segment Routing (FASR), assigns rescue vehicle types based on
continuous flood probability per road segment, replacing binary passability classification and targeting ≤3-second
route generation latency on production servers.
- The project targets a ≥40% reduction in rescue coordination time versus the audited baseline, and will deliver ≥2
Q1 publications, ≥2 IP filings, and ≥2 master's graduates upon completion of the 24-month field validation at Nghệ
An.
